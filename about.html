Our Story

Welcome to Taste Corner

Delicious Food, Friendly Staff, Cozy Atmosphere & Positive Emotions!

Founded in 2003 by Chef Andrew, our doors opened with a simple goal: To bring slow-cooked family recipes to the heart of the city. What started as a humble street-corner kitchen has grown into a gathering place for food lovers, families, and friends. We believe great food starts with respect for the ingredients. That’s why we partner with spice markets every single morning to source fresh hand-picked spices. Every dish that leaves our kitchen is made from scratch with the same care we’d serve at our own family table. Whether you are dropping by for a quick lunch, celebrating a milestone, or settling in for a long weekend dinner, we are thrilled to have you here.

Our Mission

To help our customers enjoy simple edible pleasures and reach their hearts through their stomachs.

Our Vision

To create an ultimate destination for every food lover in town and lead them to an extraordinary food journey.

Tasty Corner is known for creating unforgettable food experiences.

We believe that food is therapy and try to put up a smile on your face with our culinary skills.
